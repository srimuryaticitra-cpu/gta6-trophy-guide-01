    Roadmap to the platinum

    Eight stages, in order, built from the official trophy descriptions. The story comes first; the grind comes last. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission — guides report there is no mission replay.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. The rest are safe — but with no mission replay reported, a manual save before each chapter is cheap insurance.

        Why the rest are safe

        Story trophies pop when you finish their mission or chapter, side-character finales pop when you finish their strand, and every collectible, activity, robbery and money trophy can be finished in free roam after the story. Nothing else on the list is tied to a one-time moment — except the meditation spot in Chapter 1, flagged above.
